# BabPls tutorial video: your first PLS-SEM model in 7 steps

Files: `media/BabPls-tutorial-en.mp4` (7:31) and `media/BabPls-tutorial-ar.mp4` (8:24), 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264, AAC stereo 48 kHz at −16 LUFS; English: offline neural narration of every caption (Piper, voice en_US-joe-medium, CC0; recording paced by the speech in `tools/video/rec.js`) over generated background music that ducks under the voice; Arabic: the same, with the voice ar_JO-kareem-medium (Piper; `voice.py` turns Latin terms, symbols and decimals into Arabic words and supplies diacritics for words the automatic diacritizer misreads; the Arabic captions are shorter than the English ones so the spoken video stays near eight minutes). Soundtrack: `python tools/video/audio.py media/BabPls-tutorial-<lang>.mp4 <lang> tutorial` after recording. Piper is not deterministic (the same line can come out a few tenths of a second longer), so keep the speech cache `tools/video/.voices/cache/` with the rendered media: rec.js and audio.py then use the same clips and the narration stays in sync (max_delay 0); after clearing it, record again before running audio.py. Subtitles in `media/BabPls-tutorial-en.srt` and `media/BabPls-tutorial-ar.srt` (same text and timing as the burned-in captions).

Recorded from the real app with `tools/video/tutorial.js` (`node tools/video/tutorial.js en|ar 8772` with the app served on that port; for the narration set `BABPLS_PY` to a Python with piper-tts), using the toolkit `tools/video/rec.js`. This file is generated by `node tools/video/tutorial_doc.js`. Data: `examples/customer_satisfaction.csv` (300 respondents, 24 variables); every number quoted in the captions is what BabPls 0.8.1 shows for it (bootstrapping with 5,000 subsamples and seed 1).

## Narration script (English)

The English video speaks these captions (with symbols and abbreviations read out by `voice.py speak`); for a human voice-over, read each chapter at a calm pace; each paragraph matches the captions of that chapter.

**0:00 · Introduction.** Title card: “BabPls · Your first PLS-SEM model in 7 steps · From importing data to an APA-style report”, then the list of the seven steps. BabPls tutorial: your first PLS-SEM model in 7 steps, from importing data to an APA-style report. What you will learn: seven steps, from the start screen to the examples and the guide.

**0:13 · 1. Start screen.** Open BabPls in your browser: no installation, no account, and your data stay on your computer. From the start screen, open a ready example, browse example data, or import your own. The tabs at the top: Data, Model, Results, and Guide & references. Prefer a desktop app? Download it for Windows, macOS or Linux. The language button next to it switches the interface to Arabic.

**0:41 · 2. Import data and check it.** Click Import data: CSV, Excel or SPSS, with the variable names in the first row. Our example: customer satisfaction, 300 respondents, 24 variables. First, check the data in the Data tab. At the top: the number of rows and variables, and the total of missing values. Then descriptive statistics for every variable: mean, median, SD, kurtosis and skewness. The Missing column counts the empty cells of each variable, such as imag_2. Click any variable to see its distribution. Missing values get mean replacement by default; you can choose casewise deletion when you run an analysis.

**1:28 · 3. Build the model (constructs, names, paths).** In the Model tab, the indicators of your data are listed in the side panel. Click the first indicator, then Shift-click the last one to select the whole block. Now drag them onto empty canvas: a construct is created with these indicators. Give it a clear name in the properties panel, then press Enter. Even faster: this button creates all remaining constructs from the name prefixes in one click. Double-click a construct to rename it. Rename the others the same way: Quality, Value, Satisfaction, Loyalty. These names are the ones you will see in the results and reports. Now the paths: choose the Path tool, click the cause, then the effect. Draw the rest of the ten hypothesised paths the same way. Each arrow runs from the predictor to the outcome it affects. Back to the Select tool, then Auto layout arranges the model from left to right. Drag any construct to tidy the drawing; the ⤢ button centres the model on the canvas. The panel confirms six constructs and ten paths: the model is ready to calculate.

**2:44 · 4. PLS-SEM and the quality criteria.** The Calculate menu lists every analysis: PLS-SEM, bootstrapping, PLSpredict, IPMA, CB-SEM, and more. Start with the PLS-SEM algorithm and keep the defaults: path weighting, mean replacement. Results come in three groups: final, quality criteria, details. Start with Outer loadings. Loadings should be at least 0.708. The weakest here is loy_3, at 0.629. Between 0.40 and 0.70, delete an indicator only if that improves reliability or AVE. Reliability (α, rho_A, rho_c) should be 0.70 to 0.95, and AVE at least 0.50. For Loyalty, α is 0.638 and rho_A 0.675, both below 0.70, but rho_c is 0.802, which is acceptable. So the assessment checklist will flag its reliability. Every AVE is above 0.50. Discriminant validity: HTMT should be below 0.85, or 0.90 for similar concepts. The highest here is 0.592. Then the structural model: path coefficients, with R² and f² in the same list. The estimates are also drawn on the model: loadings, path coefficients and R². Zoom in with the + button (or Ctrl + mouse wheel) and drag the background to pan. For example, Satisfaction → Loyalty is 0.356, and Loyalty’s R² is shown under its name.

**4:31 · 5. Bootstrapping: significance and mediation.** To test significance, run Bootstrapping: 5,000 subsamples, percentile intervals, two-tailed at 0.05. The computation runs on your computer; the progress bar is at the bottom and you can cancel at any time. Each subsample is drawn with replacement and the model is re-estimated on it. 5,000 re-estimates give each estimate a standard error, a t value and a confidence interval. The random seed is fixed at 1, so running it again gives the same results. A path is significant when P < 0.05 and the 95% confidence interval excludes zero. All ten paths are significant; for example Satisfaction → Loyalty = 0.356, t = 7.34. Specific indirect effects test mediation: every chain of paths with its significance. For example Image → Satisfaction → Loyalty = 0.051, P = 0.009: a significant mediation.

**5:38 · 6. Report: checklist, APA write-up, export.** The assessment checklist checks every criterion against Hair et al. (2019). A tick is fine, a triangle needs a look, a cross is a problem. A triangle here: four loadings below 0.708. A cross for Loyalty’s reliability: its rho_A is below 0.70. And a triangle for sample size: 300 is just under the 305 needed for the smallest path. Below it, a draft APA-style write-up built from your numbers. Check it, adapt it to your study, then copy it with the Copy button. Excel exports every table of the analysis to one workbook. Export report saves a complete HTML report with all tables and charts. Data and models autosave in the browser; Save downloads the full project file with its results.

**6:37 · 7. Examples gallery, user guide and methods.** In Guide & references, open All examples: ready data for every analysis in BabPls. Each example has a guide: how to run it, how to interpret it, and what it shows. Run this analysis loads its data and model and opens it with its settings. And here is the result: the simple slopes show how support buffers the effect of workload on stress. The user guide covers data, model building, every analysis and reporting. Methods shows the formulas and sources of each method, with about 90 references.

**0:41 · 2. استيراد البيانات وفحصها.** اضغط «استيراد بيانات»: CSV أو Excel أو SPSS، والأسماء في الصف الأول. مثالنا رضا العملاء: 300 مستجيب و24 متغيراً. في الأعلى: الصفوف والمتغيرات والقيم المفقودة. ثم الإحصاءات الوصفية لكل متغير. وعمود «المفقود» يعدّ القيم الناقصة، مثل imag_2. انقر على أي متغير لعرض توزيعه. تُعالج القيم المفقودة بـ«الإحلال بالمتوسط»، أو بـ«حذف الحالة كاملة».

**1:27 · 3. بناء النموذج (المتغيرات الكامنة والأسماء والمسارات).** في لسان النموذج: مؤشراتك في القائمة الجانبية. انقر أول مؤشر، ثم Shift مع آخره لتحديد المجموعة. واسحبها إلى مساحة فارغة: يُنشأ متغير كامن يضمّها. اكتب له اسماً في لوحة الخصائص، ثم Enter. أو أسرع: هذا الزر ينشئ البقية دفعة واحدة. انقر مرتين على المتغير لإعادة تسميته. وسمِّ البقية بالطريقة نفسها. وستظهر هذه الأسماء في النتائج. الآن المسارات: اختر أداة «مسار»، وانقر السبب ثم النتيجة. وارسم بقية المسارات العشرة. كل سهم يتجه من المؤثِّر إلى المتأثِّر. ثم أداة التحديد، و«ترتيب تلقائي» يرتّب النموذج. اسحب المتغيرات لتحسين الرسم، وزر ⤢ يلائمه مع اللوحة. ستة متغيرات كامنة وعشرة مسارات: النموذج جاهز.

**2:53 · 4. خوارزمية PLS-SEM ومعايير الجودة.** قائمة «احسب» فيها كل التحليلات. ابدأ بخوارزمية PLS-SEM بإعداداتها الافتراضية. النتائج في ثلاث مجموعات. ابدأ بنموذج القياس: «التشبعات الخارجية». التشبع 0.708 فأكثر، وأضعفها هنا loy_3: ‏0.629. بين 0.40 و0.70 لا تحذف المؤشر إلا إن حسّن الثبات أو AVE. الثبات بين 0.70 و0.95، وAVE لا يقل عن 0.50. لـ«الولاء»: ألفا 0.638 وrho_A ‏0.675، دون 0.70؛ وrho_c ‏0.802 مقبول. فستعلّمه قائمة التقييم. وكل قيم AVE فوق 0.50. الصدق التمييزي: HTMT دون 0.85، وأعلاها هنا 0.592. ثم النموذج الهيكلي: المسارات وR² وf². والنتائج مرسومة على النموذج أيضاً. كبّر بزر +، واسحب الخلفية لتحريك الرسم. مثلاً: المسار الرضا ← الولاء ‏0.356، وR² لـ«الولاء» تحت اسمه.
